Phone Storage Full? 10 GB You Can Free in 10 Minutes

👤 TechPulse Team 📅 2026-10-03 ⏱ 5 min read
🎯 Difficulty: Beginner ⏱ Fix time: 10 min 💰 Cost: Free

64 GB phones fill up fast, but the biggest offenders are not your photos — they are WhatsApp media, Telegram cache, and app data you forgot existed. Here is how to find and delete all of it safely.

1. See what is actually using space

Settings → Storage → Internal shared storage

Tap each category. "Other" or "System" being huge usually means app caches.

2. Clear WhatsApp media

/storage/emulated/0/Android/media/com.whatsapp/WhatsApp/Media/

Inside: WhatsApp Images, WhatsApp Video, WhatsApp Documents, and the notorious Sent folder which duplicates everything you send. This alone is often 5–15 GB.

3. Clear Telegram downloads

Telegram → Settings → Data and Storage → Storage Usage → Clear Cache

4. Use Google Photos "Free up space"

Google Photos → Tap your profile → Free up space

This deletes local copies of photos already backed up. Usually frees 3–8 GB.

5. Clear hidden app caches

Settings → Apps → [app] → Storage → Clear cache

Do this for Facebook, Instagram, Spotify, Chrome, and Maps. Never use "Clear data" unless you want to log in again.

6. Remove duplicate files

Apps like Files by Google have a "Duplicates" and "Large files" section that finds multi-GB videos you forgot about.

7. Move photos to an SD card

Camera app → Settings → Storage location → SD card

Summary

WhatsApp media, Google Photos local copies, and Telegram cache are the three biggest wins. Ten minutes of cleanup typically frees 8–15 GB.
