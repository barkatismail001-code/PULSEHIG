Webcam Not Working? Fix Black Screen, Privacy Shutter and Drivers

👤 TechPulse Team 📅 2026-10-03 ⏱ 5 min read
🎯 Difficulty: Beginner ⏱ Fix time: 10 min 💰 Cost: Free

You joined a meeting and your camera is black. Before reinstalling Windows, check the privacy shutter — a surprising number of "broken" webcams are just physically covered or blocked by a security setting.

1. Check the physical shutter

Many laptops and Logitech webcams have a sliding privacy shutter. Slide it open. Also check for a keyboard shortcut (Fn + F8 or similar) that disables the camera.

2. Check Windows camera permissions

Settings → Privacy & security → Camera → Camera access → On
Let apps access your camera → On

Then scroll down and enable access for the specific app (Zoom, Teams, Chrome).

3. Test in the Camera app

Open the built-in Windows Camera app. If it works there but not in Zoom, the problem is the app, not the webcam.

4. Close other apps using the camera

Only one app can use a webcam at a time. Chrome tabs, OBS, and even Skype can hold it. Close them all and retry.

5. Reinstall the driver

Device Manager → Cameras → right-click → Uninstall → Reboot

6. Check USB bandwidth

A 1080p60 webcam needs a dedicated USB 3.0 controller. Plugging it into a hub shared with an external SSD causes dropped frames or a black image. Use a rear port directly on the motherboard.

7. Antivirus blocking

Kaspersky, Bitdefender and Avast all have "webcam protection" features that silently block access. Check their settings.

8. Linux: check v4l2

v4l2-ctl --list-devices
sudo apt install guvcview
guvcview

Summary

Open the shutter, grant camera permission, close other apps, and reinstall the driver. If the Camera app works but Zoom does not, the issue is the app.
