Smart TV Slow and Laggy? Speed It Up Without Buying a New One

👤 TechPulse Team 📅 2026-10-03 ⏱ 5 min read
🎯 Difficulty: Beginner ⏱ Fix time: 15 min 💰 Cost: Free–€40

A 2019 smart TV has the CPU of a 2015 phone. It will never be fast — but you can make it usable by freeing storage, killing auto-start apps, and letting a €30 streaming stick do the heavy lifting.

1. Check free storage

Settings → Device Preferences → Storage

Smart TVs often ship with 4–8 GB total. If you are below 500 MB free, apps will lag. Uninstall anything you do not use weekly.

2. Disable auto-start apps

Many TV brands preload Netflix, YouTube and shopping apps that run at boot. Look for "Auto Start" or "Startup Apps" in settings and disable all of them.

3. Turn off motion smoothing

Picture → Motion → Auto Motion Plus → Off

The interpolation engine runs on the same SoC as the UI, stealing CPU cycles from menus.

4. Update the firmware once

Then disable auto-update. Firmware updates on old TVs often add features that slow the UI further.

5. Use an external streaming stick

This is the real fix. A Fire TV Stick 4K or Google TV Chromecast has a faster CPU than most 2019 TVs. Plug it into an HDMI port, use the TV only as a monitor, and the UI becomes instant.

6. Disable "Quick Start"

Quick Start keeps the TV half-awake to boot faster, but it also keeps apps in memory and slows the UI. Turning it off adds 3 seconds to boot and saves 200 MB of RAM.

Summary

Free storage, kill auto-start apps, disable motion smoothing, and use an external stick. A €30 dongle turns a slow smart TV into a fast one without replacing the panel.
