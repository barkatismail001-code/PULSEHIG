SSD Getting Slower? Check TRIM, Over-Provisioning and Firmware

👤 TechPulse Team 📅 2026-10-03 ⏱ 6 min read
🎯 Difficulty: Intermediate ⏱ Fix time: 25 min 💰 Cost: Free

SSDs do not slow down because they are old — they slow down because the controller runs out of clean NAND blocks. TRIM, over-provisioning and firmware are the three levers that keep an SSD fast for years.

1. Benchmark first

Use CrystalDiskMark or on Linux:

sudo hdparm -tT /dev/nvme0n1
sudo fio --name=randwrite --ioengine=libaio --rw=randwrite --bs=4k --size=1G --runtime=60

Compare against the drive's rated specs. A modern NVMe should do 3000+ MB/s sequential and 200k+ IOPS random.

2. Verify TRIM is enabled

Windows:

fsutil behavior query DisableDeleteNotify
# 0 = TRIM enabled, 1 = disabled
fsutil behavior set DisableDeleteNotify 0

Linux:

sudo systemctl status fstrim.timer
sudo fstrim -av

3. Check the SATA/NVMe link speed

# Windows
Get-PhysicalDisk | Select FriendlyName, MediaType, BusType

# Linux
sudo lspci -vv | grep -i "LnkSta"

If a SATA III SSD negotiates SATA II (3 Gb/s), replace the cable — a damaged cable forces the fallback.

4. Reserve over-provisioning space

Leave 10% of the drive unallocated in Disk Management. The controller uses it for garbage collection. On a 1 TB drive, that is 100 GB that buys you years of consistent performance.

5. Update the firmware

Samsung Magician, Crucial Storage Executive, and WD Dashboard all offer firmware updates. Several models had firmware bugs that caused write speeds to collapse after 50% fill.

6. Keep at least 15% free

An SSD above 90% full loses 50%+ of its write performance. If you cannot free space, add a second drive.

7. Check for DRAM-less drives

Cheap SSDs without DRAM cache slow down dramatically once the SLC cache is exhausted. If you see a drive drop from 2000 MB/s to 80 MB/s after writing 30 GB, that is the cause — no fix, only replacement.

Summary

Enable TRIM, verify the link speed, update firmware, keep 15% free and reserve 10% over-provisioning. Those five steps keep an SSD fast for its entire life.
