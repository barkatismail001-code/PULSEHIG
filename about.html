About TechPulse

TechPulse publishes practical, step-by-step guides for people who build and repair things: ESP32 and Arduino projects, electronics troubleshooting, PC and network problems, and home repair.

What we publish

  • Troubleshooting guides. Each guide follows the same structure: the problem, possible causes, tools and components needed, a detailed step-by-step solution, images or diagrams where they help, and common mistakes.
  • Calculators. Free engineering calculators for electronics, embedded and home projects.
  • Project guides. Small home-automation projects with parts lists and wiring notes.
  • Study notes. Independent engineering study notes in the Academy section. They are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or copied from any university.
  • Parts suggestions. Recommendations based on datasheets, specifications and community experience. Prices are approximate and change often.

Editorial standards

  • Articles are reviewed by a person before they are published. Nothing is published automatically.
  • Guides must contain real values, units and part numbers, and enough detail to be followed without guessing.
  • Anything involving mains voltage, gas, water pressure or heat includes a safety note. If a repair is unsafe for a beginner, we say so.
  • We do not publish sponsored content disguised as advice.
  • We correct mistakes quickly and note significant corrections on the article.

Advertising disclosure

The site may display ads from Google AdSense. Advertisers do not influence what we write or recommend.
