About TechPulse

TechPulse is a dedicated engineering platform for embedded systems, microcontrollers, home automation, and hardware tutorials.

What we publish

  • ESP32, ESP8266, STM32, and Arduino tutorials with tested code
  • Home automation and DIY guides with real costs and time estimates
  • University-level engineering courses — MIT, Stanford, Berkeley, CMU — with lectures, assignments, and step-by-step solutions
  • 30+ engineering calculators for electronics, embedded, and home projects
  • Independently tested product picks (microcontrollers, tools, smart home devices)
  • Programming guides (Python, C, Embedded C, Git, APIs, IoT)
  • Live industry news from Hacker News, Reddit, and curated sources

Our standards

Every article is technically reviewed, uses real component values and units, and is structured with FAQ sections for AI citation. We do not publish sponsored content disguised as reviews. Our product picks are based on independent research and community feedback.

Our stack

  • Frontend: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS (fast, no frameworks)
  • Backend: Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Storage)
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages + Cloudflare CDN
  • AI: Groq API (via Supabase Edge Functions)
  • Analytics: Plausible / Umami (privacy-friendly)
