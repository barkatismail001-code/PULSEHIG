About TechPulse

TechPulse is a small independent site that explains how to fix things at home and how to use technology to make a home smarter. We cover Home Repair, Smart Tech (ESP32, Arduino, electronics, smart home), Auto, Tools & Gear and Tips & Savings.

How a guide is built

Every repair guide opens with an At a glance box: skill level, estimated time, estimated cost, tools required, parts, and a safety level. Then come the symptoms and likely causes, numbered steps with photos or diagrams, how to check the result, and the common mistakes. Electronics projects add a parts list, wiring and code you can copy.

What you will find

  • Guides and diagnoses — symptom, cause and fix, with safety warnings first.
  • Projects — ESP32 and Arduino builds with parts, wiring and working code.
  • Reviews — honest pros, cons and a clear verdict.
  • Tech News — live headlines from Hacker News and DEV that link to the original sources.

Read how we work in our Editorial Policy.

A word of caution

Repairs involving mains electricity, gas, pressure vessels or vehicle safety systems can be dangerous. Our guides are for general information. If you are not sure you can do a job safely, hire a qualified professional.
