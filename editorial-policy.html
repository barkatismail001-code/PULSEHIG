Editorial Policy

Last updated: November 2026

TechPulse exists to help you solve a real problem and do it safely. This page explains the standards we write to.

How we write

  • Guides are written from practical experience and checked against manufacturer manuals, datasheets and standards where they exist.
  • Where we have not tested something ourselves, the article says so.
  • We give real values (pin names, torque, voltage, part numbers) instead of vague advice, and we show photos or diagrams where they help.
  • Safety notes come before the step they apply to.

What the “At a glance” labels mean

  • Skill level — Beginner: no special experience. Intermediate: you are comfortable with hand tools or basic electronics. Advanced: experience is needed and mistakes are costly.
  • Estimated time and cost — realistic figures for a first-time job, based on typical parts prices. They vary by country and supplier.
  • Safety level — Low: ordinary care. Medium: risk of injury or damage if you ignore the warnings. High: mains electricity, gas, pressure, fuel or safety systems; if you are not sure, hire a qualified professional.

Reviews

A review states its pros, its cons and a clear verdict. We say whether we bought the product ourselves. Ratings are never sold, and nobody can pay for a better score.

Corrections

If you find an error, email contact@pulsehig.com. We fix confirmed mistakes quickly and show an “Updated” date on the article.

Advertising and affiliate links

At the moment TechPulse shows no advertising and earns nothing from product links. If that changes, ads and affiliate links will be clearly labelled and will never influence what we recommend or how we rate a product.

News

The news bar and news page show headlines from Hacker News and DEV. They are not written by us; each link opens the original source.
