⏱ 1 min read

Overview

The knapsack problem asks you to choose items to maximise value under a weight limit. It appears in budgeting, cargo loading, and resource allocation.

Key ideas

  • Greedy approach: pick the best value/weight ratio first.
  • Brute-force: try all subsets.
  • Dynamic programming: pseudo-polynomial, exact.

Worked example

Greedy fails: capacity 10, items (6,6),(5,5),(5,5). Greedy picks 6/6; optimal picks two 5/5.

Common pitfalls

  • Assuming greedy is optimal.
  • Ignoring the weight budget.

Why it matters

Not every problem has a fast exact solution.

Overview

Real data is noisy. Probability gives us a language for uncertainty.

Key ideas

  • Random variables, distributions.
  • Independence vs dependence.
  • Law of large numbers.

Worked example

Roll two dice 10,000 times. Average sum is ~7. Error scales as 1/sqrt(N).

Common pitfalls

  • Confusing correlation with causation.

Why it matters

Every later topic rests on probability.

⏱ 1 min read

Overview

A random walk is a path of random steps. Monte Carlo estimates quantities by sampling.

Key ideas

  • Simulate many walks.
  • Error ~ 1/sqrt(N).
  • Used in physics and finance.

Worked example

Estimate pi: N random points, pi ~ 4*(inside/total).

Common pitfalls

  • Too few samples gives high variance.

Why it matters

Complex integrals can be estimated by simulation.

Overview

Distributions describe how likely each outcome is.

Key ideas

  • Discrete: Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson.
  • Continuous: uniform, normal.
  • Maximum likelihood.

Worked example

8 heads in 10 flips gives MLE p=0.8.

Common pitfalls

  • Assuming normality without checking.

Why it matters

Inference is how we learn from data.

Overview

The simplest predictive model: fit a line to data.

Key ideas

  • Minimise squared error.
  • Normal equations.
  • Regularisation prevents overfitting.

Worked example

Slope = cov(x,y)/var(x). Intercept = mean(y) - slope*mean(x).

Common pitfalls

  • Extrapolating beyond range.

Why it matters

Regression is the entry to supervised learning.
