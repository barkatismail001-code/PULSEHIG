Overview

Bridge analog to digital design.

Key ideas

  • MOSFETs as switches; CMOS as logic.
  • Digital signal is a voltage range.
  • Noise margins make it reliable.

Worked example

3.3V logic high might be 2.4-3.3V. Above 2.0V is 1.

Common pitfalls

  • Assuming exact voltages.

Why it matters

Ladder: transistors -> gates -> modules -> processor.

Overview

Execute instructions in parallel by overlapping stages.

Key ideas

  • Forwarding handles most.

Worked example

Common pitfalls

  • Forgetting pipeline fill/drain.

Why it matters
