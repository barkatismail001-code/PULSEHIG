Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

TechPulse (pulsehig.com) respects your privacy. This page explains what is collected when you visit the site.

1. What we collect

Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymisation to see which pages are read and how visitors find the site. It uses cookies to tell visits apart. It does not tell us who you are.

Article views. When you open an article, a counter in our database goes up by one (once per browser session). No personal data is stored with it.

Contact form. If you write to us, we receive your name, email address and message through Web3Forms, and we use them only to reply.

Your browser. Your light/dark theme choice is stored in your browser (localStorage). It never leaves your device.

2. Third-party services

  • Supabase — database and image storage for articles.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting of the site.
  • Google Analytics — anonymised statistics.
  • Hacker News and DEV — the news bar and news page load headlines directly from their public APIs, so your browser contacts those services. Links to stories lead to external websites with their own policies.
  • Web3Forms — delivers contact-form messages.

3. Advertising

The site does not currently show advertising. If that changes (for example with Google AdSense), this policy will be updated before ads appear and will explain the cookies involved.

4. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser extension that blocks analytics; the site keeps working. To ask about your data, email contact@pulsehig.com.

5. Children

TechPulse is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
