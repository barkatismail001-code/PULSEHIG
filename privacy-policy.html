Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

At TechPulse, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This policy describes the types of information we collect and how we use it.

Log Files

TechPulse follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. Information collected includes IP addresses, browser type, ISP, date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and clicks. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like many websites, TechPulse uses cookies to store information about visitor preferences and to record which pages the visitor accessed. This information helps us optimize the user experience by customizing our content based on your browser type and other information.

Local Storage

Our site uses your browser's localStorage to remember your theme (light/dark), language preference, bookmarked articles, and article view history. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible or Umami) that do not use cookies, do not collect personal data, and are GDPR-compliant. Only aggregated, anonymous statistics are collected.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email address securely with Buttondown. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. We never sell or share subscriber lists.

Authentication

Account creation and forum login are handled by Supabase Auth. Passwords are hashed on the server; we never see your plaintext password. Sessions are stored in your browser as secure cookies.

Third-Party Services

Our Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We recommend consulting the respective privacy policies of third-party services we use:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, storage
  • Buttondown — newsletter subscription
  • Disqus — comments on forum and articles
  • Cloudflare — CDN and DDoS protection
  • Groq — AI features (translations, writing assistant)
  • Web3Forms — contact form processing

CCPA/GDPR Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we collect very little personal data, most requests can be handled through your browser settings (clear cookies, clear localStorage) or by emailing contact@pulsehig.com.

Children's Information

We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child provided such information on our website, contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it from our records.

Consent

By using our website, you consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on the homepage. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@pulsehig.com.
