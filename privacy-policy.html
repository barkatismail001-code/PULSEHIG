Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

TechPulse (“we”, “us”) operates pulsehig.com. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit the site, how it is used, and what choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Server log data: like most websites, our hosting and CDN providers record IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages visited and date/time. This data is used for security, troubleshooting and aggregate statistics.
  • Account data: if you create an account to use the forum or Q&A, we store your email address and the content you post. Passwords are hashed and never visible to us.
  • Contact form data: name, email and message you send us, used only to reply to you.
  • Newsletter data: your email address, if you subscribe.

2. Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: keeping you signed in (authentication session) and remembering your consent choice.
  • Preferences: your browser’s localStorage remembers your theme (light/dark), language, saved articles and reading history. This data stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.
  • Advertising: see section 3.
  • Comments: our comment system (Disqus) may set its own cookies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features of the site may not work without them.

3. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may display ads served by Google and its advertising partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: we ask for your consent before using cookies or similar technologies for personalized advertising or measurement, through a consent management platform. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the privacy settings link shown on the site.

4. Analytics

We count visits to the site and to individual articles (for example the visit counters shown in the page footer). These counters are stored as aggregate numbers in our database and are not used to identify individual visitors. If we use a third-party analytics tool, it will be one that reports aggregate statistics only.

5. Newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is stored with our newsletter provider, Buttondown. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. We never sell or share subscriber lists.

6. Third-party services

We rely on the following services, each with its own privacy policy. This policy does not cover their practices.

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage
  • Cloudflare — CDN and security
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Disqus — comments
  • Buttondown — newsletter
  • Web3Forms — contact form processing
  • Google (Gemini) — AI features such as translation. Text you submit to AI features may be processed by Google.
  • Google AdSense — advertising (see section 3)

7. Affiliate links

Some pages, such as Best Picks, may contain affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are always disclosed on the page where they appear and do not change our recommendations.

8. User-generated content

Comments, forum posts and Q&A answers are public. Do not post personal information you do not want others to see. We may remove content that is spam, abusive or unlawful.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, the UK and California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email contact@pulsehig.com. We do not sell personal information.

10. Data retention and security

We keep account and contact data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable technical measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

11. Children’s privacy

TechPulse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest revision. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@pulsehig.com
