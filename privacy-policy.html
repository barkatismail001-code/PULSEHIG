Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

TechPulse (“we”, “us”) operates www.pulsehig.com. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit the site, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Log data: like most websites, our hosting and CDN providers log IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and date/time of the request.
  • Preferences stored on your device: theme, saved articles and reading history are kept in your browser’s local storage and never leave your device.
  • Account data: if you create an account for the forum or Q&A, your email address and display name are stored with our authentication provider (Supabase). Passwords are hashed; we never see them.
  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, your email address is stored with Buttondown. Every email contains an unsubscribe link.
  • Contact form: messages you send are processed by Web3Forms and delivered to our inbox.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage for essential functions (sign-in sessions, language/theme preferences) and, with your consent where required, for analytics and advertising. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused service that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. We only see aggregated statistics such as page views and referrers.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may show advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used. Without consent, only non-personalized ads may be shown.

Comments

Comments on articles are provided by Disqus. Disqus may set its own cookies; see the Disqus privacy policy.

Service providers

  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare — hosting, content delivery and DDoS protection
  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage
  • Buttondown — newsletter delivery
  • Web3Forms — contact form processing
  • Plausible — cookie-free analytics
  • Disqus — article comments
  • Google AdSense — advertising (when enabled)

Each provider has its own privacy policy and is responsible for its own data practices.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Email contact@pulsehig.com and we will respond within 30 days. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@pulsehig.com or our contact page.
