1. The Problem the User Faces required

What exactly does the reader see? When does it happen, and what have they already tried? Be specific (error messages, symptoms, device model).

2. Possible Causes of the Problem required

List the likely causes, most probable first, with one line explaining why each one produces the symptom.

3. Tools and Components Needed required

Tools with sizes/ratings, and parts with model numbers or values.

4. Step-by-Step Solution (in detail) required

At least 3 numbered steps. Give real values, pin names, measurements and the expected result of each step.

5. Images and Diagrams add where they help

Upload your own photos, wiring diagrams or screenshots. Each image needs alt text. Do not use copyrighted images from other websites.

6. Common Mistakes required

Typical errors people make with this repair, and how to avoid or fix each one.
